06 / MY WORKFLOWFROM QUESTION TO BUILD

Explore. Build.
Debug. Repeat.

The goal isn’t to pretend I know everything. It’s to work carefully enough that every iteration teaches me something and the next one gets better.

01
⌕

Explore

Ask a clear question, research the constraints, and define what a useful first version might do.

02
⌘

Prototype

Make an early version real—whether that means a UI, a model experiment, a circuit, or a storyboard.

03
⤨

Debug

Test the assumptions, inspect the failures, and fix the small details that stand between idea and function.

04
↗

Ship & reflect

Deploy or demonstrate what works, document the limits, and turn what I learned into the next iteration.
