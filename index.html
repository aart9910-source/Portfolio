04ABOUT THE BUILDER

I learn best
when I’m making.

— AYAN / LEARNING BY BUILDING

I’m a student developer who enjoys following a question wherever it leads: into a model training run, a web interface, a motor controller, or a creative experiment.

I’m not trying to present every idea as a finished product. I care about understanding how the pieces work, debugging what doesn’t, and sharing what I’ve learned along the way.
